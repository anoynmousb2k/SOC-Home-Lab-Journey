# SOC 101 Fundamentals: Theory & Core Concepts

Source: TCM Security Academy — *Security Operations (SOC) 101* (`https://youtu.be/56NDgBOSpUg`)

## 1. The SOC and Its Role
- **Purpose:** Central function responsible for continuous monitoring, detection, investigation, and response to security events across an organization's environment.
- **Key Responsibilities:** Triage alerts, analyze suspicious activity, coordinate incident response, and help reduce dwell time and risk.
- **How It Connects to My Lab:** Understanding alert triage and detection workflows shapes how I configure logging, dashboards, and rules later in my lab.

## 2. Information Security Foundations
- **Core Principles:** Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA triad) and how defensive controls protect business operations.
- **Defense Mindset:** Security controls work in layers—prevention, detection, and response all play distinct roles.

## 3. Common Threats and Attacks
- **Threat Landscape:** Key attack types, vectors, and adversary behaviors analysts commonly encounter in alerts and telemetry.
- **Analyst Takeaway:** Recognizing what normal vs. malicious activity looks like makes log analysis and alert investigation much faster.
